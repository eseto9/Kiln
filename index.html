KILN

forty windows of stained glass

How to play

Each window is a grid of empty glass. Drag across cells to cut a pane. Let go and the pane either sets in colour, or cracks.

The rules

Every window states its rule at the top. A pane only sets if it obeys every rule shown.

  • Shape — every pane must be the shape shown. Rotated or mirrored both count.
  • Cells — every pane must have exactly that many cells.
  • Gem — every pane must hold exactly one gem.
  • Frame — every pane must touch the outer frame.
  • Dark windows hide gems and lead until glass is set beside them. Cut into hidden lead and the pane cracks.

Heat

The kiln bar at the top is your life. Cracks heat it (+5 per cell). Setting glass cools it (−1 per cell). When the window is full, it fires. Fire early with cells left and each one heats the kiln (+4), and every cracked cell costs +2 more. A window with no cracks cools −8 extra. Heat carries across all forty windows. If it ever reaches the top, the glass shatters and the run ends.

Boons and cathedrals

After every window, draft one of three boons. They stack for the whole run and some pairs unlock a synergy. Every fifth window is a cathedral window: bigger, two rules at once. Clear it and its relic is yours for every run after.

Tips

  • Dark cells with a dot are lead. They belong to no pane and cannot be cut.
  • A tap on one cell does nothing. Drag back over your last cell to undo it.
  • Leaving one awkward cell empty is usually cheaper than cracking four.

Relics

One relic from a cleared cathedral window may be carried into each run.
