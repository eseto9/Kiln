KILN

forty windows before nightfall

How to play

You are a glazier with one day of light. Each window is a grid of empty glass. Drag across cells to cut a pane. If it obeys the window's rule it sets in colour. If not, it dissolves.

The rules

  • Shape — every pane must be the shape shown. Rotated or mirrored both count.
  • Cells — every pane must have exactly that many cells.
  • Gem — every pane must hold exactly one gem.
  • Frame — every pane must touch the outer frame.
  • Dark windows hide gems and lead until glass is set beside them.

Light

The light bar is your day. Nothing on the board can hurt you, but light is spent:

  • Undo a pane: −1 light. Z on a keyboard.
  • A pane that dissolves: −2.
  • Each empty cell when the window fires: −4.
  • Every window fired: −2, the day moving on.
  • A flawless window, every cell set: +5.

When the light runs out, night falls and the run ends. The sky behind the glass tells you how late it is.

Boons and cathedrals

After every window, draft one of three boons. They stack for the whole run and some pairs unlock a synergy. Every fifth window is a cathedral window: bigger, more rules. Clear it and its relic is yours for every run after.

Tips

  • Cells with a dark stud are lead. They belong to no pane.
  • A tap on one cell does nothing. Drag back over a cell to remove it from the cut.
  • Undo is cheap. Empties are not. Lift a bad pane rather than fire around it.

Relics

One relic from a cleared cathedral window may be carried into each run.
